Best battery: go big

Plus and Pro Max models have bigger batteries. The iPhone 16 Plus and 16 Pro Max are battery champions. If you're a heavy user, size up.

Cheapest worth buying: iPhone SE (3rd gen)

Same A15 chip as the iPhone 13 in a small body with a home button and Touch ID. Great first iPhone for kids. The trade-offs: small screen, one camera, shorter battery life.

[[products:iphone-se-3rd-gen]]

Models we'd skip now

  • iPhone 11 and older: fine for basic use, but closer to the end of updates. Only at a big discount.
  • iPhone SE (2nd gen): older chip and weaker battery. The 3rd gen is worth the extra.
  • Any 64GB iPhone if you shoot video: you'll run out of space fast.

How much storage?

  • 128GB: right for most people with iCloud Photos on.
  • 256GB: if you shoot a lot of video or keep photos on the phone.
  • 512GB+: creators and 4K video shooters.

FAQ

What's the best iPhone to buy for the money?

A refurbished iPhone 13 or iPhone 15. Both offer great cameras, battery and years of updates for far less than a new iPhone.

Which iPhones support Apple Intelligence?

iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, and every iPhone 16 and newer model, including the iPhone 16e.

Is it worth buying an iPhone 12 or 13 now?

Yes, at the right price. They're still fast and supported. The 13 has the better battery.
