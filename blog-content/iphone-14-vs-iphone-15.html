Buy the iPhone 15 if you want USB-C, the 48MP camera and the Dynamic Island. Buy the iPhone 14 if you just want a great iPhone for less money. Day to day, they feel closer than Apple's marketing suggests.

The short version

The iPhone 15 is the iPhone 14 with three real upgrades: a USB-C port, a 48MP main camera, and the Dynamic Island instead of the notch. The size, weight and battery life are basically the same.

So the question isn't "which is better". The 15 is better. The question is whether those three things are worth the price gap to you.

Camera: the biggest real difference

The iPhone 15's 48MP sensor is the upgrade you'll actually notice. Photos have more detail, and the 2x zoom uses the middle of that big sensor, so zoomed shots look sharp instead of mushy. The 14 only has digital zoom past 1x.

In good light, both take great photos. Indoors and at night, the 15 holds detail better. Portraits are better on the 15 too, since you can turn a normal photo into a portrait after you take it.

If you mostly shoot your friends, food and the odd sunset, the 14 is still very good. If you zoom a lot or care about detail, get the 15.

USB-C vs Lightning

The 15 charges with the same USB-C cable as your laptop, iPad, Android-using friends and most new earbuds. One cable for everything. The 14 still uses Lightning.

Don't expect faster file transfers. The base iPhone 15's port runs at USB 2 speed, same as Lightning. The win is convenience, not speed.

Also handy: the iPhone 15 can charge AirPods or an Apple Watch through the cable.

Screen and Dynamic Island

Both screens are 6.1-inch OLED and both run at 60Hz. Neither has the smooth 120Hz scrolling of the Pro models.

The 15 gets much brighter outdoors (2000 nits vs 1200). In direct sun, that's the difference between reading your map and squinting at it.

The Dynamic Island replaces the notch. It shows timers, music, rides and deliveries in a little pill at the top. It's nice. It won't change your life.

Battery and performance

Battery life is a tie on paper and close to a tie in real use. Both get through a normal day.

The A16 in the 15 is a bit faster, but the A15 in the 14 is still quick for apps, games and video. You won't feel lag on either for everyday use.

Software updates

Apple usually supports iPhones for six or more years. The 15 came out a year later, so it should get roughly one extra year of updates. If you keep phones for a long time, that's a point for the 15.

Who should buy which

Get the iPhone 14 if…

  • You want the lowest price on a modern iPhone
  • You already own Lightning cables and accessories
  • Your photos are mostly casual
  • It's a first iPhone or a phone for a teen

Get the iPhone 15 if…

  • You want one cable for all your devices
  • You zoom, shoot at night, or care about detail
  • You're outside a lot and need a bright screen
  • You keep phones 4+ years

FAQ

Is the iPhone 15 worth it over the iPhone 14?

Yes if you value USB-C, a better camera with 2x zoom, and a brighter screen. If you don't care about those, the iPhone 14 gives you nearly the same daily experience for less.

Do the iPhone 14 and 15 support Apple Intelligence?

No. Apple Intelligence needs an iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or any iPhone 16 or newer.

Is the iPhone 15 the same size as the iPhone 14?

Almost. Both have 6.1-inch screens. The 15 has slightly rounder edges and weighs about the same. Most iPhone 14 cases don't fit the 15 because the camera bump is different.

Which has better battery life?

They're rated the same: up to 20 hours of video playback. In real use, they're very close.
