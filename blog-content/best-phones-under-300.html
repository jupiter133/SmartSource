How to spot a fake deal

  • Check the launch year. A cheap phone that's out of updates is not a deal.
  • Ask about battery health. Under 80% means you'll be paying for a battery soon.
  • Look for a real warranty. "Tested" means nothing without one. Ours is a full year.
  • Make sure it's unlocked and clean. No carrier lock, no previous owner's account, no blacklisted IMEI.
  • Watch storage. 64GB fills up fast. 128GB is the sweet spot.
