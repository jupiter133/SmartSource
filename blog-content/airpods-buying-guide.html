Quick picks

  • Best for most people: AirPods Pro. Best noise cancelling, best fit, great sound.
  • Best open-fit: AirPods 4 with ANC. No ear tips, surprisingly good noise cancelling.
  • Cheapest: AirPods 4 (without ANC).
  • Over-ear: AirPods Max.

AirPods 4: if ear tips bug you

AirPods 4 sit in your ear without silicone tips, so they feel lighter and less "plugged". The ANC version does a solid job on bus and office noise, though not as well as the Pro, since there's no seal.

AirPods Pro: the one most people should get

The seal from the ear tips makes the noise cancelling and bass much better, and they stay put during workouts. AirPods Pro 2 also gained hearing test and hearing aid features. AirPods Pro 3 improved noise cancelling and fit further, and added heart rate tracking for workouts.

A refurbished Pro 2 is the value pick. The Pro 3 is the best if you want the latest.

AirPods Max: over-ear

Great sound and noise cancelling, premium build, but heavy and pricey, and the case is more of a sleeve. Worth it if you want over-ear and live in Apple's world.

Not on iPhone?

AirPods work with Android as basic Bluetooth earbuds, but you lose auto-switching, easy setup, battery widgets and most settings. Android users usually get more from Galaxy Buds or Pixel Buds.

Buying refurbished AirPods: what to check

  • Battery health. AirPods batteries can't be cheaply replaced, and they wear like phone batteries. Make sure they've been tested for battery life.
  • New ear tips. For hygiene, Pro models should come with fresh tips.
  • Genuine. Fake AirPods are everywhere. Check the serial number on Apple's coverage site.
  • Unpaired. They should be removed from the previous owner's Apple Account so Find My doesn't block pairing.
  • Warranty. A must for earbuds.

Make them last

  • Keep the case clean and dry. Clean the mesh with a dry, soft brush.
  • Don't leave them in a hot car.
  • Turn on Optimized Battery Charging (on by default with an iPhone).

FAQ

Are refurbished AirPods safe and hygienic?

From a proper refurbisher, yes. They should be cleaned, tested, and Pro models should come with new ear tips.

AirPods 4 or AirPods Pro?

AirPods Pro for the best noise cancelling and a secure fit. AirPods 4 if you find ear tips uncomfortable.

How long do AirPods batteries last?

Most AirPods noticeably lose battery life after a few years of daily use. That's why battery testing matters when buying refurbished.
