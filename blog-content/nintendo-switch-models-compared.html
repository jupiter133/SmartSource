Switch 2: for new games

The Switch 2 has a much bigger, sharper screen, a lot more power, and 256GB of storage. New Nintendo games like Mario Kart World and Donkey Kong Bananza are Switch 2 only. It plays most original Switch games too, and many run better. The trade-off is shorter battery life and a higher price.

Switch OLED: best value

The OLED screen is the best thing about it: deep blacks, bright colours, and it's bigger than the original. It has a better kickstand, double the storage, and a dock with a wired internet port. For the thousands of games already out on Switch, this is the sweet spot.

Switch Lite: for kids and handheld play

Smaller, lighter and cheapest. Controllers are built in, so there's nothing to lose. The catches: it can't connect to a TV, and some games that need detachable Joy-Cons are awkward or need extra controllers. Perfect for kids and travel.

Original Switch: only at the right price

It does everything the OLED does with a smaller, duller LCD. If you buy one, make sure it's the 2019 model (model number HAC-001(-01) on the back) with the much better battery.

FAQ

Can the Switch 2 play original Switch games?

Yes, most original Switch games work on Switch 2, physical and digital. A small number have compatibility issues, so check Nintendo's list for a specific game.

Can the Switch Lite connect to a TV?

No. The Switch Lite is handheld only.

Is the Switch OLED worth it over the regular Switch?

Yes for most people. The bigger, brighter OLED screen, better kickstand and double storage make a real difference.
